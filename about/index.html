My Journey: From Wrenches to Code

For over a decade, my world was defined by physical systems—the hydraulics of an aircraft tow tractor, the sensors of a scientific instrument, the massive machinery of a cement plant. I'm an engineer at heart, and I've always loved understanding how things work.

But a passion kept growing: a fascination with the code that controls these systems. I realized the future of engineering is written in software. So, I decided to rebuild my career from the ground up, transitioning from the physical to the digital.

I'm now a dedicated student of technology, honing my skills in programming (Python, JS), data (SQL, Pandas, Power BI), and web development. My current mission is to merge my past and future: to become a developer who doesn't just write code, but understands the real-world industrial problems that code can solve.

This portfolio is a logbook of my journey. Here you'll find my projects—from data analysis scripts and web apps to automation tools and IoT prototypes. Each one is a step towards my goal: to build intelligent systems that bridge the gap between the physical and digital worlds.

I'm actively looking for a junior developer, data analyst, or automation engineer role where I can contribute, learn intensely, and help solve tough problems.
